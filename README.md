# Ink Supplies Portfolio Simulation

A portfolio project demonstrating the analytical framework used to manage a ~$1.9B global ink supplies portfolio at a major consumer hardware company.

## Live site

**[chrisacannon.github.io/ink-portfolio-simulation](https://chrisacannon.github.io/ink-portfolio-simulation)**

## What this is

This project models the assumption framework I maintained across ~55 platform × region × business model combinations — the inputs that drove financial planning, NPI evaluation, and demand forecasting for a global ink supplies business.

**Landing page (`index.html`):** A plain-language explainer of the four revenue drivers that govern any hardware-plus-consumables business (installed base, usage, pricing, aftermarket share), and the core NPI decision framework they powered.

**Simulation (`simulation.html`):** The detailed assumption model — cartridge pricing, yield, mix, aftermarket share decay, and NPI vs do-nothing unit economics — across four business models and multiple regions.

## Notes

- Product specifics have been abstracted to protect confidentiality
- The model structure, assumption logic, and economic relationships reflect the actual analytical framework used in the role
- Known areas for future refinement: NPI supplies revenue calculation across all business model / region combinations; aftermarket decay chart Y-axis scaling in high-OEM-share regions
